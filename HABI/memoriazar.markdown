## subjunctive mood
### use when: 
- we want to express a wish or preference, or give advice and make suggestions.
- the situations are UNREAL.
- we are speaking as if we controlled the situation.
### remember:
- Were - NOT was => “If I were, you were, he/she/it were” etc.
- There is no third person singular S.
- Request, insist, recommend, demand, prefer
## make logical deductions using modal verbs
- use MUST when we are convinced that something must be true.
- use MIGHT, MAY, CAN and COULD when things seem less obvious or sure.
- CAN is used mainly for general possibilities
- COULD is used for weak possibilities.
- CAN’T and COULDN’T are both used for things we believe are impossible.
## Since can be used in two different ways
- used to mean after and relates to time.
- used to mean because or as, and introduces reason.

## What types of polite requests can we have?
- COMMAND: Ask your client if she can see it displayed in the chat section correctly. // Please collect the logs yourself.
- OBLIGATION STATEMENT: I need you to ask the customer for the logs.
- SUGGESTION: You should ask the customer to take screenshots.
- QUERY: Could you ask your client if she can see it displayed in the chat section properly? // Can you please ask the customer for the logs? // Why don’t you collect the logs yourself?
- PREFERENCE: I think asking the customer for the logs is the best option.
- HINT: I was wondering if you might be able to ask your client if she can see it displayed in the chat section correctly. // It would be best if you collected the logs yourself. // I'm so thirsty!
